Engine · Week 2, about 85% volume

Home version

  1. Prep, about 6 min (on the bike day, this is the bike warm-up too):
    • Air bike 3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Bear crawl, 2 x 5 m forward and back. Knees an inch off the floor, opposite hand and foot together.
  2. Light play, 5–8 min: pogos 2 x 15, then med ball chest pass 2 x 5. Crisp and easy, not a workout.
  3. Air bike 3 x 4 min at RPE 8–9, 3 min easy spin between. Ramp first: 2 min easy, then 2 x 15 s building to work pace. Same watts on every round. When every round feels like RPE 8, add about 5% next time. Builds to 4 rounds by week 4.
  4. Cool-down: 3–5 min easy spin, nasal breathing.

Short on time or legs fried: 2 x (10 x 30 s hard / 30 s easy), 3 min between blocks.

Gym version

  1. Prep, about 6 min (on the bike day, this is the bike warm-up too):
    • Air bike or rower 3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Bear crawl, 2 x 5 m forward and back. Knees an inch off the floor, opposite hand and foot together.
  2. Light play, 5–8 min: pogos 2 x 15, then med ball chest pass to the wall 2 x 5. Crisp and easy, not a workout.
  3. Air bike or rower 3 x 4 min at RPE 8–9, 3 min easy between. Ramp first: 2 min easy, then 2 x 15 s building to work pace. Same watts (or split) on every round. When every round feels like RPE 8, add about 5% next time. Builds to 4 rounds by week 4.
  4. Cool-down: 3–5 min easy, nasal breathing.

Short on time or legs fried: 2 x (10 x 30 s hard / 30 s easy), 3 min between blocks.
