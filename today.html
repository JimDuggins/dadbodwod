Monday, Nov 9, 2026

Easy · Week 2, about 85% volume

No loads here: pick weights that leave the listed reps in reserve (RIR). Full templates: Home plan · Gym plan.

Home version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Banded shoulder external rotation, 1 x 12/side. Elbow pinned to your side, slow on the way back.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Bear crawl, 2 x 5 m forward and back. Knees an inch off the floor, opposite hand and foot together.
  2. Play, 10 min: pogos 2 x 15, then med ball throws (overhead or rotational), 2 x 5. Quick, fun, full reset between.
  3. Upper pump, 10 min, 2 rounds at RIR 2, 10–15 reps each: ring push-up, ring row (feet elevated), banded lateral raise, band curl or pushdown. First round a touch easier as your ramp.
  4. Easy aerobic, 30 min: air bike, brisk incline walk or ruck. Talk test: you could speak in sentences but would rather not.

Short on time: keep the aerobic block; cut the pump to one round.

Gym version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Band or cable shoulder external rotation, 1 x 12/side. Elbow pinned to your side, slow on the way back.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Bear crawl, 2 x 5 m forward and back. Knees an inch off the floor, opposite hand and foot together.
  2. Play, 10 min: pogos 2 x 15, then med ball throws against a wall (overhead or rotational), 2 x 5. Quick, fun, full reset between.
  3. Upper pump, 10 min, 2 rounds at RIR 2, 10–15 reps each: push-up or machine chest press, cable or ring row, cable lateral raise, cable curl or rope pushdown. First round a touch easier as your ramp.
  4. Easy aerobic, 30 min: bike, incline treadmill walk or rower. Talk test: sentences, but you'd rather not.

Short on time: keep the aerobic block; cut the pump to one round.

General programming for healthy adults, not medical advice. If you have an injury, a medical condition, or pain, check with a qualified professional before you start. Train at your own risk and scale anything that hurts.
