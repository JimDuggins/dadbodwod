Friday, Nov 6, 2026

Upper · Week 1, about 85% volume

No loads here: pick weights that leave the listed reps in reserve (RIR). Full templates: Home plan · Gym plan.

Home version

Warm-up, 3 min: 90/90 breathing, dead bug, bird dog.

  1. Play, 8 min max: rotational slam 3 x 3/side, then med ball chest pass 3 x 5. Full intent on every rep, full reset between.
  2. Weighted bar dip, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Build depth and load gradually. Pause briefly at the bottom; ribs down.
  3. Weighted pull-up, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Full hang to chest-to-bar or as high as clean reps allow. No kipping.
  4. Arms/shoulders, superset, 2 x 10–15: banded lateral raise + ring curl.
  5. Core: kettlebell side bend 2 x 10/side, start light. Or landmine rotation if you prefer.

Short on time: drop the arms superset first, then the core.

Gym version

Warm-up, 3 min: 90/90 breathing, dead bug, bird dog.

  1. Play, 8 min max: rotational med ball throw (wall or slam) 3 x 3/side, then med ball chest pass to the wall 3 x 5. Full intent, full reset.
  2. Weighted dip, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Build depth and load gradually. Pause briefly at the bottom; ribs down. Barbell or dumbbell bench is the swap if dips aren't available.
  3. Weighted pull-up, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Full hang to chest-to-bar or as high as clean reps allow. Lat pulldown if you can't get 5 clean yet.
  4. Arms/shoulders, superset, 2 x 10–15: cable or dumbbell lateral raise + cable or dumbbell curl.
  5. Core: dumbbell side bend 2 x 10/side, start light. Or landmine rotation / cable woodchop.

Short on time: drop the arms superset first, then the core.

General programming for healthy adults, not medical advice. If you have an injury, a medical condition, or pain, check with a qualified professional before you start. Train at your own risk and scale anything that hurts.
