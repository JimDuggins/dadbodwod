Lower · Week 2, about 85% volume

Jump to Home version · Gym version

No loads here: pick weights that leave the listed reps in reserve (RIR). Full templates: Home plan · Gym plan.

Home version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Glute bridge hold, 2 x 20 s. Push through the whole foot, ribs down, no arching.
    • Bodyweight lateral lunge, 1 x 5/side. Sit back into the hip, stay tall.
  2. Play, 8 min max, 15–25 contacts: broad jump 3 x 3 (stick the landing, walk back), pogos 2 x 10. Full intent, full reset.
  3. Hex bar, high handles, sit deep, 3 x 4–6, RIR 1–2. Two or three ramp sets first. Belly breaths between sets.
  4. Rear-foot split squat with kettlebells, 2 x 6–10/leg, RIR 1–2. One light ramp set per leg.
  5. Nordic curl, 2 x 4–6, slow lowering. Feet anchored under something heavy; use a small push-off at the bottom.
  6. Suitcase carry, 2 x 30–40 m/side. Tall, ribs down, no lean.

Short on time: cut the carry to one round, then the Nordics.

Gym version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Glute bridge hold, 2 x 20 s. Push through the whole foot, ribs down, no arching.
    • Bodyweight lateral lunge, 1 x 5/side. Sit back into the hip, stay tall.
  2. Play, 8 min max, 15–25 contacts: broad jump 3 x 3 (stick the landing, walk back), pogos 2 x 10. Full intent, full reset.
  3. Hex/trap bar, high handles, sit deep, 3 x 4–6, RIR 1–2. Two or three ramp sets first. Belly breaths between sets.
  4. Rear-foot split squat with dumbbells, 2 x 6–10/leg, RIR 1–2. One light ramp set per leg.
  5. Nordic curl on a GHD or Nordic bench, 2 x 4–6, slow lowering. Small push-off at the bottom.
  6. Suitcase carry (dumbbell or kettlebell), 2 x 30–40 m/side. Tall, ribs down, no lean.

Short on time: cut the carry to one round, then the Nordics.
