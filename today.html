Upper · Week 2, about 85% volume

Jump to Home version · Gym version

No loads here: pick weights that leave the listed reps in reserve (RIR). Full templates: Home plan · Gym plan.

Home version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Banded shoulder external rotation, 1 x 12/side. Elbow pinned to your side, slow on the way back.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Bear crawl, 2 x 10 steps forward and back. Knees an inch off the floor, hips level.
    • Two or three light ramp sets of the press before the work sets.
  2. Play, 8 min max: rotational slam 3 x 3/side, then med ball chest pass 3 x 5. Full intent on every rep, full reset between.
  3. Kettlebell press, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Double or single bell, strict: no leg drive. Set the rack hold first: wrist straight, bell on the forearm, not hanging off it. Forearms hurting? Barbell strict press cleaned from the floor.
  4. Single-arm kettlebell row, braced on the bench, 3 x 5–8/side, RIR 1–2. Pause at the top, no torso twist.
  5. Arms/shoulders, superset, 2 x 10–15: banded lateral raise + ring curl.
  6. Core: ab wheel 2 x 6–10. Ribs down, stop before your lower back sags.

Short on time: drop the arms superset first, then the core.

Gym version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike (or any bike) 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Band or cable shoulder external rotation, 1 x 12/side. Elbow pinned to your side, slow on the way back.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Bear crawl, 2 x 10 steps forward and back. Knees an inch off the floor, hips level.
    • Two or three light ramp sets of the press before the work sets.
  2. Play, 8 min max: rotational med ball throw against a wall (or slam) 3 x 3/side, then med ball chest pass to the wall 3 x 5. Full intent, full reset between.
  3. Standing barbell strict press, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Glutes tight, ribs down, no leg drive, bar finishes over mid-foot. Cranky shoulders? Seated dumbbell press or landmine press.
  4. Chest-supported row (dumbbells or machine), 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Pause at the top. Alternative: single-arm dumbbell row.
  5. Arms/shoulders, superset, 2 x 10–15: cable lateral raise + cable curl.
  6. Core: ab wheel 2 x 6–10. Ribs down, stop before your lower back sags.

Short on time: drop the arms superset first, then the core.
