HARD Bike Power + Lower · Week 2, about 85% volume

Jump to Home version · Gym version

No loads here: pick weights that leave the listed reps in reserve (RIR). Full templates: Home plan · Gym plan.

Home version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Glute bridge hold, 2 x 20 s. Push through the whole foot, ribs down, no arching.
    • Bodyweight lateral lunge, 1 x 5/side. Sit back into the hip, stay tall.
  2. Bike power (the fast block), 4 x 5–6 s fast but not all-out, 2–3 min easy spin between. Ramp first: 4–5 min easy spin with 3 gradual pickups (about 5 s each at roughly 60%, 70%, 80%). Seated, rolling start, smooth acceleration. Quality over volume; stop early if the last rep is clearly slower.
    • Optional running block, instead of the bike, never both: only with daylight, a field, turf or track, and dry, secure footing. Graded running warm-up with build-ups around 60%, then 4 x 40 m relaxed strides around 70%, full walk-back rest. Otherwise ride. No makeup day.
  3. Hex bar, low handles, 2 x 3–5, RIR 2. This is the hex bar week (last Saturday was split squats). Ramp first: 3–4 sets of 5, 3, 3, building to the work weight. Belly breaths between sets.
    • Back cranky? High handles, 2 x 4–5 at RIR 3, instead.
  4. Suitcase carry, 2 x 30–40 m/side. Tall, ribs down, no lean.

Short on time: keep the bike block, cut the carry to one round.

Gym version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike (or any bike) 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Glute bridge hold, 2 x 20 s. Push through the whole foot, ribs down, no arching.
    • Bodyweight lateral lunge, 1 x 5/side. Sit back into the hip, stay tall.
  2. Bike power (the fast block), 4 x 5–6 s fast but not all-out, 2–3 min easy spin between. Air bike or any bike. Ramp first: 4–5 min easy spin with 3 gradual pickups (about 5 s each at roughly 60%, 70%, 80%). Seated, rolling start, smooth acceleration. Quality over volume; stop early if the last rep is clearly slower.
    • Optional running block, instead of the bike, never both: only with daylight, a field, turf or track, and dry, secure footing. Graded running warm-up with build-ups around 60%, then 4 x 40 m relaxed strides around 70%, full walk-back rest. Otherwise ride. No makeup day.
  3. Hex/trap bar, low handles, 2 x 3–5, RIR 2. Front squat 2 x 3–5 from the rack is the swap. Ramp first: 3–4 sets of 5, 3, 3, building to the work weight. Belly breaths between sets.
    • Back cranky? High handles, 2 x 4–5 at RIR 3, or reverse hyper 2 x 12–15, instead.
  4. Suitcase carry, 2 x 30–40 m/side with a dumbbell or kettlebell. Tall, ribs down, no lean.

Short on time: keep the bike block, cut the carry to one round.
