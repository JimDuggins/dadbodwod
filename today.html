Upper · Week 2, about 85% volume

Jump to Home version · Gym version

No loads here: pick weights that leave the listed reps in reserve (RIR). Full templates: Home plan · Gym plan.

Home version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Banded shoulder external rotation, 1 x 12/side. Elbow pinned to your side, slow on the way back.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Scap pull-up, 1 x 6. Dead hang, pull the shoulder blades down without bending the elbows, 2 s hold, slow return.
  2. Play, 8 min max: rotational slam 3 x 3/side, then med ball chest pass (or explosive push-up) 3 x 5. Full intent on every rep, full reset between.
  3. Weighted dip, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Ramp first: bodyweight x 5, then a light plate x 3, then work sets. Build depth and load gradually; brief pause at the bottom, ribs down.
  4. Weighted pull-up, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Ramp first: bodyweight x 3–5, then a light plate x 2–3, then work sets. Full hang to chest up, no kipping.
  5. Arms/shoulders, superset, 2 x 10–15: banded lateral raise (strict, no bounce) + ring curl.
  6. Core: kettlebell side bend 2 x 10/side, start light. Slow, no lean forward or back.

Short on time: drop the arms superset first, then the core.

Gym version

  1. Prep, about 6 min:
    • Air bike (or any bike) 2–3 min: 30 s moderate / 30 s easy. Not intervals, just warm.
    • Band or cable shoulder external rotation, 1 x 12/side. Elbow pinned to your side, slow on the way back.
    • Dead bug with a long exhale, 1 x 6/side. Low back stays heavy on the floor.
    • Scap pull-up, 1 x 6. Dead hang, pull the shoulder blades down without bending the elbows, 2 s hold, slow return.
  2. Play, 8 min max: rotational med ball throw against a wall (or slam) 3 x 3/side, then med ball chest pass to the wall 3 x 5. Full intent, full reset between.
  3. Weighted dip, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Ramp first: bodyweight x 5, then a light plate x 3, then work sets. Brief pause at the bottom, ribs down. Barbell or dumbbell bench is the swap (ramp the same way).
  4. Weighted pull-up, 3 x 5–8, RIR 1–2. Ramp first: bodyweight x 3–5, then a light plate x 2–3, then work sets. Lat pulldown if you can't get 5 clean reps yet.
  5. Arms/shoulders, superset, 2 x 10–15: cable or dumbbell lateral raise + cable or dumbbell curl.
  6. Core: dumbbell side bend 2 x 10/side, start light. Or landmine rotation / cable woodchop.

Short on time: drop the arms superset first, then the core.
