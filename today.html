Saturday, Nov 7, 2026

Sprints + short Lower · Week 1, about 85% volume

No loads here: pick weights that leave the listed reps in reserve (RIR). Full templates: Home plan · Gym plan.

Home version

Warm-up, 3 min plus a longer build if it's cold: 90/90 breathing, dead bug, bird dog, then skips, A-skips and a few easy strides.

  1. Sprints (the play block): fly sprints on turf or a track, 4 x (20 m build + 10 m fly) at about 85–90%. Smooth and relaxed, full walk-back rest (2–3 min). 150 m of fast running max. Then 4 deceleration or 5-10-5 shuttle reps.
    • Bad weather: hill sprints if it's dry, 4–6 x 6–8 s, walk down. If it's wet or icy, air bike 10 x 6 s all-out with 54 s easy.
    • Stop when speed drops, form breaks down, or a hamstring feels off.
  2. Heavy rear-foot split squat, 2 x 5–6/leg, RIR 2. Heavy kettlebell in one hand or a barbell. One ramp set first. This week is the split-squat week; next Saturday is low-handle hex bar.
  3. Suitcase carry, 2 x 30–40 m/side. Tall, ribs down, no lean.

Short on time: keep the sprints, cut the carry to one round.

Gym version

Warm-up, 3 min plus a longer build if it's cold: 90/90 breathing, dead bug, bird dog, then skips, A-skips and a few easy strides.

  1. Sprints (the play block): fly sprints on turf or a track, 4 x (20 m build + 10 m fly) at about 85–90%. Smooth and relaxed, full walk-back rest (2–3 min). 150 m of fast running max. Then 4 deceleration or 5-10-5 shuttle reps.
    • Bad weather: curved treadmill 4–6 x 6 s all-out, 2–3 min full rest. Or air bike 10 x 6 s all-out with 54 s easy.
    • Stop when speed drops, form breaks down, or a hamstring feels off.
  2. Heavy rear-foot split squat, 2 x 5–6/leg, RIR 2. Dumbbells, a heavy kettlebell or a barbell from the rack (front squat 2 x 3–5 is the swap). One ramp set first. Next Saturday is low-handle hex bar.
  3. Suitcase carry, 2 x 30–40 m/side with a dumbbell or kettlebell. Tall, ribs down, no lean.

Short on time: keep the sprints, cut the carry to one round.
