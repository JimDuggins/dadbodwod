## How it works
- **Every session: fast, then heavy, then accessory.** Jumps, throws or sprints first while you're fresh, then one or two heavy lifts, then a little size work.
- **Templates, not menus.** Each day has fixed slots. Pick one exercise per slot from a short list, then keep that pick for 3–4 weeks.
- **Only two hard days.** Thursday (bike intervals) and Saturday (bike sprints / bike power). Everything else is done short of failure. Running replaces the bike only on a Saturday with daylight and dry, secure footing on a field, turf, or track. Never both, and no makeup day.
- **Leave reps in the tank.** Working sets stop 1–2 reps short of failure (RIR 1–2). Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then add load.
- **Minimum effective dose.** About 10 hard sets per muscle per week, one interval day, one bike-sprint day, a little easy cardio.

## Disclaimer
General programming, not medical advice. If you have an injury, a medical condition, or pain, consult a qualified professional before starting. Train at your own risk.
