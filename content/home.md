# Home plan (garage)
<p class="lede">Move fast, lift heavy, breathe hard. About 45 minutes, Monday to Saturday, two hard days.</p>

## Equipment
Barbell and plates, hex bar, kettlebells (a light, a medium and a couple of heavy ones), flat bench, pull-up/dip bar, rings, bands, ab wheel, med balls and slam balls, air bike, a low box. Turf or a track for Saturday sprints. No rack needed.

**Missing something?**
- **No hex bar:** barbell deadlift from blocks, or a heavy kettlebell deadlift/goblet squat.
- **No rings:** suspension straps, or bar rows under a sturdy table or low bar.
- **No air bike:** any bike, a rower, or hill runs for the intervals.
- **No med or slam balls:** kettlebell swings and broad jumps for the play block.
- **No dip bars:** two sturdy chairs for bodyweight dips, or loaded push-ups.
- **No bench:** row with a hand on a box or chair; press standing.

## The week
| Day | Type | Session (~45 min) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Easy | Play 10 + upper pump 10 + easy aerobic 30 |
| Tue | Moderate-hard | **Lower** (heavy) |
| Wed | Moderate | **Upper** (overhead press, row) |
| Thu | <span class="tag">HARD</span> | Light play + bike 4x4 |
| Fri | Moderate | **Upper** (dip, pull-up) |
| Sat | <span class="tag">HARD</span> | Sprints + short **Lower** |
| Sun | Off | |

**Every session:** 3-minute warm-up (90/90 breathing, dead bug, bird dog), then fast, then heavy, then accessory. Slow belly breaths between heavy sets.

## Day templates
Default pick in **bold**. Keep a pick 3–4 weeks; swap only if it stalls or hurts.

### Mon: Easy
1. **Play, 10 min:** pogos, throws, skips, anything quick and fun.
2. **Upper pump, 10 min, 2–3 rounds at RIR 2:** ring push-up, ring row (feet elevated), banded lateral raise, band curl or pushdown.
3. **Easy aerobic, 30–45 min:** bike, brisk incline walk, or ruck. Talk test: you could speak in sentences but would rather not.

### Tue: Lower
1. **Play, 2 picks, 15–25 contacts, 8 min max:** **broad jump**, **pogos**, bounds, single-leg hops, lateral bounds, low drop jump, slam ball throw.
2. **Squat pattern, 4 x 4–6, RIR 1–2:** **hex bar, high handles, sit deep**; barbell front squat (clean it up) when ready.
3. **Single leg, 3 x 6–10/leg:** **rear-foot split squat with kettlebells**, step-up, lateral lunge.
4. **Posterior chain, 2–3 sets:** **Nordic curl** (heels under a loaded bar on a pad; build slowly), single-leg hip thrust, kettlebell RDL.
5. **Carry/core:** **suitcase carry**, hanging leg raise (tuck the pelvis first). No side bends on heavy hinge days.

### Wed: Upper (vertical push / horizontal pull)
1. **Play, 2 picks, 8 min max:** **rotational slam**, **med ball chest pass**, overhead slam, explosive ring push-up, kettlebell clean.
2. **Push, 4 x 5–8:** **kettlebell press** (double or single); barbell strict press cleaned from the floor if the rack hold bothers your forearms.
3. **Pull, 4 x 5–8:** **single-arm kettlebell row, braced on the bench.**
4. **Arms/shoulders, 2 picks, 2–3 x 10–15:** **banded lateral raise**, **ring curl**, kettlebell overhead extension, band face pull, band pushdown.
5. **Core:** **ab wheel**, or hanging leg raise.

### Thu: Engine <span class="tag">HARD</span>
1. **Light play, 5–10 min:** pogos, throws.
2. **Bike 4 x 4 min at RPE 8–9, 3 min easy between.** Same watts on all four. When all four feel like RPE 8, add about 5% next week.
3. **Short on time or legs fried:** 2 x (10 x 30 s on / 30 s off).

### Fri: Upper (horizontal push / vertical pull)
1. **Play, 2 picks, 8 min max:** same list as Wednesday.
2. **Push, 4 x 5–8:** **weighted bar dip** (build depth and load gradually); loaded ring push-up as the alternative.
3. **Pull, 4 x 5–8:** **weighted pull-up.**
4. **Arms/shoulders, 2 picks, 2–3 x 10–15:** same list as Wednesday.
5. **Core:** **kettlebell side bend 2 x 10/side, start light**, or landmine rotation. Hanging leg raise either day: tuck the pelvis first, lead with the knees, no swinging.

### Sat: Sprints + short Lower <span class="tag">HARD</span>
1. **Sprints (this is the play block):** fly sprints on turf or a track, 4–5 reps, 150 m of fast running max, full rest. Start smooth at 85–90% (about 20 m build + 10 m fly) and build over weeks toward 95%. Add 4–6 deceleration or 5-10-5 shuttle reps on turf.
   - **Winter fallback:** hill sprints if dry; otherwise bike sprints, 10 x 6 s all-out with 54 s easy.
   - **Stop** when speed drops, form breaks down, or a hamstring feels off.
2. **Short lift, ~20 min:** alternate weeks between **hex bar, low handles, 3 x 3–5 at RIR 2** and **heavy rear-foot split squat 3 x 5–6**. Then **suitcase carry 2–3 x 30–40 m/side.**
   - **Back cranky?** Kettlebell swing 3 x 10 instead of the hex bar.

## Weekly targets (hard sets)
Push ~10–12, pull ~10–12 (counting Monday pump), squat/single leg ~10, hinge/posterior ~7–9, side delts ~5–7, arms ~5–7 each, plus carries and core.

## Rules
- **Effort:** working sets at RIR 1–2 (1–2 reps left in the tank).
- **Progression:** double progression. Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then add load and drop to the bottom of the range.
- **No rack, light gear?** Progress with tempo, pauses, deficits and single-leg work before load.
- **Start easy:** about 85% of the listed sets for weeks 1–3 (usually one fewer set on the main lifts), then build.
- **New hinge or ballistic work:** only add more after two weeks with no worse next-morning stiffness or pain.
- **If your low back flares:** cut swings first, then heavy hinge to 2 sets at RIR 3, and skip Nordics that week.
- **Deload:** when you see fatigue signs (lingering soreness, slower sprints, poor sleep, aches that stick around), or every 6–8 weeks anyway. Cut sets 40–50%, keep the loads.
- **Short on time:** cut the arms/shoulders pick first, then core. Never cut the fast block.

## Why it looks like this
- About 10+ hard sets per muscle per week drives size (Schoenfeld 2017; Pelland 2024).
- Mixing cardio and lifting costs little size or strength (Schumann 2022).
- 4x4 intervals for VO2max (Helgerud 2007). Short bike sprints as a winter fallback (Umeå HIT study, 2023).
- Regular near-top-speed sprinting may protect hamstrings (Malone; Edouard).
- Varied, full-intent jumps and throws for athletic carryover (coach opinion).
- For back pain, exercise helps and no single type wins (Hayden 2021, Cochrane).
